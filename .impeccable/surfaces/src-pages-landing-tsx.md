# Landing surface brief

## Scope and mode

Persuade: the first-time visitor should understand the product's clarity-from-chaos mechanism and start a study kit.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Students studying from dense notes or source material. They need to see that Notefox/StudySpark organizes confusion into a useful study path. Primary action is starting a study kit; secondary action is exploring the public library. Preserve HashRouter navigation, auth return paths, real library content, and no invented customer proof. No phone mockup or scroll hijacking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The study kit is a clarity workbench: messy source enters at the left, the next useful learning move is marked at the right; refuse the centered screenshot-and-feature-card SaaS arrangement.
OWN-WORLD: Mineral paper, deep evergreen ink, one persimmon signal, graphite rules, and printed index-strip surfaces; Space Grotesk-style compact sans hierarchy with one 16px surface radius, 10-12px controls, and signal marks for action/state.
STORY: A student sees source fragments become a guide, recall card, and practice prompt, believes the product will reduce study friction, then starts a kit or explores the library.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned promise and two clear actions occupy the left half; a functional clarity board occupies the right with source snippets, a guide outline, a flashcard, and a practice question linked by one signal rail; native page scroll follows.
FORM: Clarity Workbench, grounded candidate 1, selected from direction seed 4b7f4d99; code-led build because the decision page could not be served in the headless environment.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
